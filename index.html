YouTube影片上傳：說明與隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-13　｜　English version below

這是什麼

「YouTube影片上傳」是頻道擁有者自己使用的個人工具，只在擁有者本人的電腦上執行，用來把擁有者自己製作的影片上傳到擁有者本人的 YouTube 頻道。它不對外提供服務，沒有其他使用者，也沒有任何伺服器或網站後台。

本工具使用 YouTube API 服務。使用本工具即表示同意 YouTube 服務條款，本工具處理資料的方式也受 Google 隱私權政策 約束。

存取哪些資料、用來做什麼

  • 擁有者本人的頻道：上傳影片（一律先設為私人）、上傳字幕與縮圖、標示 AI 合成內容；讀取影片處理狀態。
  • 擁有者本人頻道的觀看數據（YouTube Analytics，唯讀）：製作擁有者自己看的每日報告。
  • 公開的搜尋結果：讀取公開影片的標題與觀看、按讚、留言數，只用來挑選影片題目，保存不超過 30 天。

本工具不讀取、不保存其他使用者的私人資料，不使用 Cookie，不做廣告或追蹤，也不把任何資料出售或提供給第三方。

資料存放在哪裡

Google 授權權杖只存在擁有者本人電腦的 macOS 鑰匙圈裡，不寫進檔案、不上傳到任何地方。其他資料也只存在擁有者本人的電腦上。

如何撤銷授權

隨時可以到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權設定 移除本工具的存取權。撤銷後，本工具就無法再存取 YouTube 帳戶。

聯絡方式

請使用 Google 授權畫面上顯示的支援電子郵件聯絡。

YouTube影片上傳: About and Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

What this is

"YouTube影片上傳" is a personal tool used only by the channel owner. It runs only on the owner's own computer and uploads videos made by the owner to YouTube channels owned by the owner. It is not offered to anyone else, has no other users, and has no servers or web back end.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and its handling of data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed and why

  • The owner's own channel: upload videos (always private first), upload caption tracks and thumbnails, set the altered or synthetic content disclosure, and read video processing status.
  • The owner's own channel analytics (YouTube Analytics, read-only): a daily report for the owner.
  • Public search results: titles and view, like and comment counts of public videos, used only to choose video topics and kept for no more than 30 days.

The tool does not read or store private data of any other user, uses no cookies, does no advertising or tracking, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Google authorization tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. They are never written to files or uploaded anywhere. All other data also stays on the owner's computer.

How to revoke access

You can remove this tool's access at any time in your Google Account third-party access settings. After that, the tool can no longer access the YouTube account.

Contact

Please use the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
